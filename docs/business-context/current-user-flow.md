# Current User Flow
### Current (Pen & Paper, Folder)
The current contract workflow is entirely **manual**. Staffs are responsible for discussing the details with the client, writing the agreement in a contract by hand, and storing the completed document in a physical folder.
![Current User Flow](.assets/user-flow-diagram.png)

1. **Verbal Discussion**
	The client and the staff discuss and agree on the key booking details, including:
	- the **reservation date**,
	- the **amenities** the client intends to use (swimming pool, function hall),
	- the **event services** they wish to avail (catering, glazing etc.),
	- and any applicable **additional fees** (e.g., corkage, excess charges, etc.).

2. **Written Agreement**
	The staff documents the agreed details in a contract, which includes section for:
	- **Client Information** (name, contact, address, etc.)
	- **Booking Details** (contract date, reserved date, occasion, etc.)
	- **Amenities** (swimming pool, function hall, rooms, etc.)
	- **Event Services** (catering, sounds & lights, etc.)
	- **Additional Fees** (corkage, excess charges, and other applicable fees)

3. **Store in a Physical Folder**
	Once the contract is finalized and signed, the staff securely files the document in a designated physical folder for record-keeping and future reference.
